# Global events and China supported-data observation

Version GLOBAL-EVENT-OBS-v1 / CHINA-SUPPORTED-OBS-v1. Observation only; no orders or execution tickets. These modules do not change account risk limits, global flags, the scheduled paper recovery or existing positions.

China strategist's scheduled entry point now clears its candidate file and runs the supported-data observer. The executor entry point returns before connecting to the database or processing candidates. The previous strategy functions remain available for reference/tests but are not the scheduled execution path. Restoring China execution requires a reviewed code change, not a permissive environment variable.

The observer records FXI/KWEB/MCHI/BABA independently with current broker eligibility, quote age/positive prices/non-crossed spread checks, completed-session history and exchange-calendar checks. Quote freshness is 120 seconds and maximum relative spread 3%. TCEHY remains explicitly unavailable; broad-stimulus evidence is null. Price-based tailwind is labelled a price proxy, not a verified policy event. A bad quote prevents selection for that instrument without erasing supported observations. Selection is hypothetical and never becomes a ticket. Its versioned records are in research/event-evidence/events.sqlite under china_observation, separated from production strategy learning.

Official event sources are listed in event_sources.json. RSS and Atom are parsed as supplied; selected official HTML listing adapters retain titles/links and mark unverified publication times missing. Feed access/parse errors and truncation are reported, never treated as a successful empty response. A source's initial import is historical context. New items qualify with a timezone-aware publication timestamp within 24 hours, not in the future. Alternatively, an explicitly dated official article from today/yesterday can support a separately labelled first-seen forecast; the missing publication time remains missing. These date-only observations cannot establish announcement-time latency or surprise. Unknown dates produce no predictions. HTML enrichment is limited to four articles per source/run; cached articles are not continuously checked for revisions. Feed changes may be revisions and remain separate evidence; they must not be treated as statistically independent events. SEC's current-filings feed is broad and capped at its latest 100 items, not comprehensive company monitoring. Coverage gaps are visible in latest.json.

OpenAI Responses API (gpt-4.1-mini-2025-04-14) classifies supplied text only. Every impact must use an allowed instrument and a verbatim evidence excerpt. Unknown consensus/surprise remain null; model direction is an unverified hypothesis. No browser/tools are attached to the model call. Source text is explicitly untrusted. The existing OpenAI client records provider, model, response ID, tokens and billed cost in the NWT system log. At most 20 attempts/day and four/run; failed attempts count. One labelled historical classification checks integration but cannot create a prediction.

Three frozen rules:

1. Delayed reaction: tentative model direction, entry at the next US regular-session open after classification plus a two-minute safety buffer; exit at the following session open.
2. Overreaction reversal: observe the first eligible full session after the event; require an absolute open-to-close move of at least 2% and volume over 1.5 times the preceding 20-session median. Freeze the opposite direction after that close and before the next open; hold one session.
3. Persistent repricing proxy: same confirmed reaction, follow its direction for five sessions. This is price confirmation, not verified surprise versus consensus.

The event watch and thresholds are recorded before the reaction session. Late activation is recorded as missed, never filled retrospectively. Outcomes use exchange-calendar open timestamps rather than daily-bar midnight timestamps, and are evaluated only after the exit open plus 15 minutes. All qualifying wins and losses are kept, alongside no-prediction reasons and non-triggered reaction checks. Delayed outcomes are compared with same-direction SPY returns over the same sessions. Assumed cost is 5 basis points per side. Shorts are hypothetical and omit borrowing, so none of these labels are executable or broker P&L. Hypotheses can conflict/overlap: no aggregation into achievable portfolio returns is justified. Daily bars may later be revised; the first evaluated outcome and supporting market snapshots are preserved.

The oversized/malformed Chinese statistics RSS source was replaced by its official release listing; historical evidence from unsuccessful attempts remains in the journal.

The official-source polling service is independent of trading halts and runs every 15 minutes on the VPS. China retains its existing weekday strategist/executor schedule. SQLite uniqueness preserves first-seen records; the pipeline has an exclusive process lock and records daily model-attempt limits before calls. Latest health files are replaceable views; historical records are append-only through the application. These are operational controls, not tamper-proof storage.

Validation: test_event_observation.py checks baseline/future exclusion, immutable first-seen data, grounded classification, invalid/stale quotes, pre-open freezing, signed returns and costs, RSS/Atom parsing, independent China observations, disabled execution and no selection while closed. Run with python3 -m unittest discover -s research -p test_event_observation.py. The installed scripts also compile before deployment. Real observations do not establish profitable predictions; forward outcomes require subsequent market sessions.
