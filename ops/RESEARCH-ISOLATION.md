# Paper production release: 27 September 2026

The official event collector is observation-only. Repository dependency inspection found its SQLite records consumed by research/event_pipeline.py and china/observation.py, neither of which produces trade tickets or broker orders. Trading engines use their own decision inputs, prices, broker receipts and operations status. The former watchdog converted all event-source failures into a global entry hold.

This release classifies only the known global-events command and its source-health snapshot as research warnings, under a deployment-time policy in /var/lib/nwt-ops/research-policy.json. Unknown commands remain critical. An event snapshot advertising execution capability is critical. Other collector, discovery, accounting, price, order and execution checks remain critical. No risk sizing, trade permissions or mutation promotion threshold changes.

Event-source failures retain bounded existing job retries and immutable records. Classification and new event hypotheses skip failed source dependencies. Already-frozen hypotheses can still be scored. Persistent research warnings notify after 30 minutes and appear in daily reports; they do not hold unrelated paper entries. Per-warning age prevents new failures resetting older ones. Historical session incidents remain intact; only newly occurring observation-only failed jobs after the exact policy effective timestamp are exempt from trading trial incidents. A clean trading trial does not certify healthy research or profitable strategy performance.

The daily collector now runs six prospective virtual portfolios: SPY and QQQ, each buy-and-hold, 200-day trend, and a 20-day breakout above that trend held for ten sessions. Initial history warms indicators only. A frozen target must precede its next regular-session open; no retrospective signals are created. Calendar gaps and missing bars never invent fills. Portfolio changes and session observations commit together in SQLite, making repeated collection idempotent. Adjusted open/close ratios model total-return exposure without reusing share quantities across changing corporate-action adjustments. Entry and exit each charge 15bps. Open exposure is marked daily, cash earns no interest, and intraday drawdowns are not measured. These estimates are not broker trades or executable $5k allocations.

The daily comparison never calls a broker order endpoint or automatically promotes a strategy. Its failure is a research warning rather than an interruption to collection/trading. Daily reports show current broker equity, strategy returns, same-symbol passive differences and drawdowns separately. Existing research comparisons and existing positions remain intact.

Validation includes bounded-warning timing, job allowlisting, old incident preservation, continuing critical engine failures, causal daily targets, missing-bar recovery, no duplicate processing, ten-session exit cost accounting and frozen-policy protection, plus the existing release suite.
